# Le Tote

> Le Tote was an online women's clothing rental and subscription-box business founded in San Francisco in 2012 by Brett Northart and Rakesh Tondon (Y Combinator S13). It acquired Lord & Taylor from Hudson's Bay Company in August 2019, filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on 2 August 2020, and was sold to Saadia Group that October. Saadia Group was reported effectively shut down in March 2024 and the letote.com website went offline that spring. **Le Tote is defunct.**

## Status

- The company is out of business. There is no live website: letote.com is still registered with IONOS but returns SERVFAIL — it delegates to Amazon Route 53 nameservers that no longer host the zone.
- Le Tote never published a public API, developer portal, or API documentation. This repository therefore contains no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, MCP, skills, scopes, authentication, conventions, or error artifacts — those absences are accurate, not gaps awaiting enrichment.
- The only public developer surface that survives is the GitHub organization, which remains readable.

## Developer surface

- [GitHub organization](https://github.com/LeToteTeam): 19 public repositories, 5 of them first-party non-forks. Last activity 2020.

## First-party packages

Internal engineering libraries published by Le Tote — not client SDKs for any Le Tote API. All unmaintained.

- [band](https://hex.pm/packages/band) (Elixir, Hex, Apache-2.0): a collection of instrumenters for common Elixir projects.
- [tm_mercury](https://hex.pm/packages/tm_mercury) (Elixir, Hex, Apache-2.0): a pure Elixir implementation of the ThingMagic Mercury RFID reader SDK.
- [unresponsys](https://rubygems.org/gems/unresponsys) (Ruby, RubyGems): an opinionated Ruby wrapper for the Responsys REST API.
- [Stardaze](https://cocoapods.org/pods/Stardaze) (Swift, CocoaPods, MIT): a Swift GraphQL query-building tool.
